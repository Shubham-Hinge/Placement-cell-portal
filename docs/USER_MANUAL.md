# 1. Introduction

The **Placement Cell Portal** is an AI-powered campus recruitment platform designed to simplify and automate the placement process for educational institutions.

The application enables four different users to collaborate through a centralized platform:

- Student
- Company
- Mentor
- Administrator

Each user receives a dedicated dashboard based on their role.

The portal provides features such as:

- Job Management
- Resume Upload
- AI Resume Analysis
- ATS Score
- Career AI Assistant
- Placement Analytics
- Mentor Assignment
- Cloud Resume Storage

# 10. AI Resume Analysis

The Placement Cell Portal includes an AI-powered Resume Analyzer using **Google Gemini AI**.

This feature helps students improve their resumes before applying for jobs.

---

## How to Analyze Your Resume

### Step 1

Login as a **Student**.

---

### Step 2

Navigate to:

```text
Student Dashboard → Resume
```

---

### Step 3

Upload your resume in **PDF** format.

---

### Step 4

Click:

```text
Analyze Resume
```

---

### Step 5

Wait a few seconds while the AI processes your resume.

---

## AI Analysis Report

The generated report includes:

* ATS Score
* Resume Summary
* Strengths
* Weaknesses
* Missing Skills
* Suggested Improvements
* Keywords Found

---

## Benefits

* Improves ATS compatibility
* Highlights missing technical skills
* Provides personalized suggestions
* Helps prepare for campus placements
* Enhances resume quality

# 13. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1. Which file format is supported for resumes?

**Answer:** PDF only.

---

### Q2. Can I upload multiple resumes?

Only one active resume is stored per student. Uploading a new resume replaces the previous one.

---

### Q3. Is resume analysis automatic?

No. Click **Analyze Resume** after uploading your resume.

---

### Q4. Can companies edit job postings?

Yes. Companies can update or delete their own job postings.

---

### Q5. Who assigns mentors?

Only the **Administrator** can assign mentors to students.

---

### Q6. Can I change my password?

Yes. Open **Settings** and update your password.

---

### Q7. Does the application work on mobile devices?

Yes. The application is fully responsive and optimized for:

* Desktop
* Tablet
* Mobile

# 14. Troubleshooting

## Login Issues

### Problem

Unable to log in.

### Solution

* Verify your email and password.
* Ensure your email has been verified.
* Use the **Forgot Password** option if required.

---

## Resume Upload Failed

### Solution

* Upload only PDF files.
* Ensure the file size is within the supported limit.
* Check your internet connection.

---

## AI Analysis Not Working

### Solution

* Confirm a resume has been uploaded.
* Retry the analysis.
* Contact the administrator if the issue persists.

---

## Dashboard Not Loading

### Solution

* Refresh the page.
* Verify your internet connection.
* Log out and log back in.

---

## Theme Not Changing

### Solution

* Refresh the browser.
* Ensure JavaScript is enabled.
* Clear browser cache if necessary.

# 15. Best Practices

To get the best experience:
